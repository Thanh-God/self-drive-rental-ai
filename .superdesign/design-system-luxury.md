DESKTOP ONLY, 1440px, content adapted to 1280–1920 desktop widths. Every page canvas, navbar, footer, filters, cards and sidebars must remain dark: base #0A0A0A; graphite surfaces #1A1A1A; gold accent #D4AF37; white body text, muted neutral gray secondary text. No sand/cream/light page backgrounds, orange/teal accents or large white panels. Photographs may be naturally bright but not the surrounding surfaces. Preserve original homepage wordmark, elegant editorial proportions and thin gold dividers. Body Montserrat, display Cormorant Garamond, Vietnamese-capable fallback. Legible body weight/contrast; serif reserved for headings, not tables/input labels. Restrained hover/focus, no compulsory motion.
Use original homepage as visual ground truth. Legacy code/css supplied describes actual functions and structure only, not the new visual direction. Same navigation/footer and shared component language across all three.
Business constraints: Vietnamese interface and VND integer amounts per 24h block, Vietnam time. Vehicle samples Toyota Vios, Hyundai Accent, Mitsubishi Xpander; do not claim Bentley/Aston/Porsche are actual project fleet. ACTIVE is Đang khai thác, NOT schedule availability. Server quote validates schedule, branch route and price. No online payment, AI/Map active features, mobile key, premium insurance guarantee, membership benefit, award, fake review score or operational actions beyond current contract.
List /vehicles: search name/brand, operational status, category, minimum seats, transmission, sort name/price. Dark photographic vehicle cards with amenities and detail links; filter clear and loading/error/retry/empty states.
Detail /vehicles/:vehicleId: proposed gallery/thumbnails and plain credit area; actual specs/amenities/current branch/description/public review section. Sidebar VND/24h and Kiểm tra giá & lịch to /user/bookings/new?vehicleId=...; dates/branches selected in that existing next step, not instant confirmation. Gallery implementation is C10B pending, model photos illustrative, no rights verification implied.
USER /user/bookings: search ID/vehicle and status filter; rows show ID, vehicle, Vietnam start/end time, pickup→return branches, total VND and Chi tiết /user/bookings/:id. Translate PENDING CONFIRMED IN_PROGRESS RETURNED COMPLETED CANCELLED REJECTED EXPIRED; no payment/analytics/staff/admin/refund/new actions.
Exactly THREE new page drafts; preserve original homepage and previous light drafts for history. No production source changes.
Use ONLY the fonts, colors, spacing, and component styles defined in this design system and the selected Luxury homepage. Do not introduce any fonts, colors, or visual styles not in this design system.
